Knitting Division

M.M. Knitwear Ltd. operates one of the largest knitting divisions in Gazipur, Bangladesh. Our knitting machines produce weft-knitted fabrics for the t-shirts, tops, hoodies, sweaters and joggers we manufacture for leading international apparel brands.

View all production divisions →

158 Knitting Machines

Our circular knitting unit has 110 machines with 20GG, 24GG and 28GG cylinders to produce all types of weft-knitted fabric, complemented by 28 flat knit machines, 6 narrow fabric machines and 14 units of fabric testing machinery. Machine brands include Fukuhara, Fukuhama and Jiunn Long.

80,000 Sq Ft Floor Space

The knitting unit occupies 80,000 square feet of floor space at our Konabari complex in Gazipur, keeping yarn sourcing, knitting, testing and quality control under one roof.

28,000 KG Daily Knitting Capacity

Our daily knitting capacity of 28,000 KG of fabric feeds our dyeing, garments, printing, AOP and embroidery divisions, enabling a fully integrated knit composite manufacturing service.
