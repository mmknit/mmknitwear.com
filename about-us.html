About Us

M.M. Knitwear Ltd. is a 100% export-oriented knit composite manufacturer established in September 2001 in Gazipur, Bangladesh. From our head office in Uttara, Dhaka and our factory at Ambagh Road, Konabari, we design and produce high-quality knitted apparel for leading international brands, independent boutiques and specialist retailers across Europe and beyond.

We aim to continue developing with the latest manufacturing technologies available to accommodate large orders and satisfy our clients well. Our strength as a private company enables us to create an organic environment that provides opportunity for our people to grow as we do. M.M. Knitwear Ltd. has already been recognised by many influential business leaders in Bangladesh and is well on its way to becoming an industrial institution in one of the world's rising economies.

  • Year of establishment: September 2001.
  • Annual turnover: 140.00 million US Dollars (2026).
  • Production capacity: 200,000 pieces/day across 116 production lines with 3,850 sewing machines.

Quality Policy

We strive to achieve customer excellence by exceeding expectations of product quality, performance and delivery. We are committed to implementing quality management systems that conform to international standards. Our drive to build a sustainable business through continuous improvement, quality management, attention to detail and a strict commitment to our buyers’s requirements continues to gain momentum. Thank you to all our clients for making this dream a reality.

Compliance

Our factory maintains full compliance with international laws and standards covering human rights, social compliance, technical compliance, C-TPAT, labour law and other rules and instructions set by the relevant authorities.

Employee & Social Commitment

As an organisation, our strength is focused on our employees and the development of their skills, enabling us to offer the best products and services to our customers. Through sponsorship, we also support local, regional and national sports and charity initiatives.

Sustainable Development

Since the beginning we have always strived to provide the best quality products to our customers that are sourced and manufactured ethically. We are continuously looking to work more sustainably to reduce plastic consumption. All our facilities strictly maintains OEKO-TEX and other compliances to reduce the use of energy consumption, waste management and safe working environment.
