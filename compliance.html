Compliance & Sustainability

M.M. Knitwear Ltd. is committed to ethical manufacturing, sustainability and social responsibility across every stage of our vertically integrated knit composite operation in Gazipur, Bangladesh. From worker welfare and fire safety to environmental protection and certified quality management, we hold ourselves to the highest international standards.

Global Accreditations

We maintain the highest international standards for quality, safety and ethical manufacturing. Our factories are certified and audited under leading global schemes, including ACCORD, OEKO-TEX, AMFORI BSCI, WRAP and SEDEX, with ISO and OHSAS management systems in place.

Sustainability Initiatives

We have set a plan to reduce 14% of our GHG emissions by the year 2029 through the following initiatives:

  • 1,160 KW of solar panel capacity on site
  • Daylight and LED lighting (reduce 0.50%)
  • Exhaust gas boiler (reduce 3.00%)
  • Reuse and recycling programmes (reduce 0.25%)
  • Sustainable product design
  • Zero waste initiatives

Corporate Social Responsibility

We are fully committed to social responsibility and worker welfare. Our comprehensive CSR programmes include:

  • Modern medical facility - 2 units with 12 beds, with full-time free service provided by two professional doctors on site
  • Child care facility for 100 children of employees
  • Biological ETP - 280 m3 for responsible wastewater treatment
  • Canteen service and subsidized lunch for employees
  • Elected workers' welfare committee to improve morale, job satisfaction and attendance
  • Fire safety systems, controls and regular training
  • 24/7 security personnel and systems
  • Regular safety and skill development training
  • In-house prayer facilities for men and women, plus an annual cultural celebration and picnic

Environmental Compliance

We follow strict environmental safety regulations to avoid pollution. Our biological effluent treatment plant (ETP) with 280,000 litres per hour capacity ensures responsible wastewater treatment. We are committed to sustainable manufacturing practices that minimise our environmental footprint, from energy-efficient machinery to waste reduction and lower plastic consumption across our facilities.
