Frequently Asked Questions

Where is M.M. Knitwear Ltd. located?

Our head office is at House 16, Road 10, Sector 01, Uttara Model Town, Dhaka, and our factory is at Ambagh Road, Nilnagar, Konabari, Gazipur, Bangladesh.

What products does M.M. Knitwear manufacture?

We are a 100% export-oriented knitwear manufacturer producing t-shirts, polo shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, pants and other knit garments for men, women and kids.

Do you work with international apparel brands?

Yes. We export to more than 10 countries and produce for brands such as Zara, Pepe Jeans, OVS, Terranova, Springfield, Ulla Popken, Crew Clothing, Sfera, Auchan and New Yorker.

How do I request a quotation or product samples?

Send your requirement to bashir@mmknitwear.com or info@mmknitwear.com, or use the contact form on this page, and our marketing team will respond with pricing and sample lead times.

What certifications does your factory hold?

Our factory is certified under ACCORD, OEKO-TEX, AMFORI BSCI, WRAP and SEDEX, and operates to ISO and OHSAS standards for quality and safety.
