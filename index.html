M.M. Knitwear Ltd. - 100% Export-Oriented Knitwear Manufacturer in Bangladesh

High Quality Clothing

All clothes are cut, stitched and finished by hand and undergo a five-stage quality control procedure to ensure your satisfaction. Throughout manufacture, all stages are quality checked to ensure the highest standards are met.

01

Fabric Sourcing

Fabrics are sourced to match your requirements for composition and texture. We can also dye fabrics to meet your exact colour specifications and apply additional treatments for softness.

02

Prototypes & Sampling

For each order we create a prototype for your approval, allowing you to test and refine the functionality of your design. Once your samples have been approved, we will commence bulk manufacture.

03

Bulk Manufacture

Bulk manufacture takes place on our production line, where all items are made for order and quality checked throughout. Quality checks also take place in the finishing stages, before your items are packaged for delivery.

04

Our Journey

Established in 2001, M.M. Knitwear is one of the top knitwear manufacturers based in Bangladesh, specialising in organic cotton and fast fashion knitwear for global brands, independent boutiques and specialist retailers. As one of the country's leading clothing manufacturers, we produce garments of the highest quality and offer a full-scale manufacturing solution built around our buyers' specifications.

Our fully integrated knit composite facility in Gazipur produces 200,000 garments per day across more than 15,23,000 square feet of factory space, supported by a workforce of over 10,982 people. Every stage of production - yarn and fabric dyeing, circular knitting, cutting, sewing, finishing, printing, AOP and embroidery - is managed in-house to protect quality, lead time and compliance.

We supply knitted apparel including men's t-shirts and pants, women's tops and dresses, and kids' hoodies and sweaters to leading international brands such as Zara, Pepe Jeans, OVS, Terranova, Springfield, Ulla Popken, Crew Clothing, Sfera, Auchan and New Yorker. Our factories are ACCORD, OEKO-TEX, AMFORI BSCI, WRAP and SEDEX certified, with ISO and OHSAS management systems in place.
